# Privacy Policy for Poly Metrics Chrome Extension

**Last Updated:** November 2025

## Data Collection and Storage

Poly Metrics extension respects your privacy. This extension:

### ✅ What We Do:
- **Local Storage Only**: All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage API
- **No Data Transmission**: The extension does not send any data to external servers
- **No User Tracking**: We do not track your browsing behavior or collect analytics

### 📦 Data Stored Locally:
The extension stores the following data **only on your device**:

1. **Settings Data:**
   - Tax rate preferences
   - Currency preferences and exchange rates
   - Default settings

2. **Bookmarks:**
   - Product page bookmarks you save
   - Category organization for bookmarks

3. **Quick Links:**
   - Quick links you create for product pages

4. **UI Preferences:**
   - Toggle states for collapsible sections
   - Panel open/closed states

### ❌ What We Don't Do:
- We do **NOT** collect personal information
- We do **NOT** send data to any server
- We do **NOT** track your browsing history
- We do **NOT** share data with third parties
- We do **NOT** use analytics or tracking services

## Permissions Used

The extension requests the following permissions:

- **`storage`**: To save your settings, bookmarks, and preferences locally on your device
- **`tabs`**: To open the settings page in a new tab when needed

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

## Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API. No data leaves your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
